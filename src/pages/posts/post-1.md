Welcome to my new blog built with Astro! 🚀

## Why Astro?

Astro makes it super fast to generate static sites from simple Markdown files without sending unnecessary JavaScript to the client.

### Key Highlights:
- **Zero JS by default**
- **Markdown & MDX support**
- **Easy file-based routing**
